Questions people ask me.

Can’t find yours? Ask me directly →
How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what you need, so I don’t publish fixed prices. After a free call, I send you a fixed-price quote with the scope, timeline and everything included, so you know the full investment before we start.

How long does it take?

A landing page or a simple prototype takes 3–7 days. A full UX/UI design for an app usually takes 2–6 weeks. For web apps, we define the timeline together on the free call, because it depends on what the app needs to do.

What do I need to get started?

Just your idea and a few minutes to tell me about it. If you have a logo, photos, texts or examples you like, great, but they’re not required. I’ll guide you through the rest.

I started my app in Lovable or Bolt, can you take it over?

Yes. Those tools are a great way to start. I audit what you built and move it to the technology it needs, free from the tool’s plans. Sometimes parts need to be rebuilt, but you end up with a working product that is yours, independent and ready to grow.

Do you build it, or only design it?

Both. I design in Figma and build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, using AI-assisted development to move fast. If your project needs a more complex back end, I’ll tell you on the first call and plan the best way to handle it with you.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Your code and domain are yours. I host projects on my Netlify account so I can keep them running and fix things fast, and if you ever want to move, I hand everything over.

Do you maintain it after launch?

Yes. Websites have an annual hosting and care plan on Netlify that includes hosting, bug fixes and small data changes, like a new phone number or opening hours. New content or new sections are quoted separately. For web apps and apps, the care plan is quoted per project.

Do you work with businesses in any industry?

Yes. I’ve worked in mental health, education, logistics, travel and events, and the process is the same in any industry: understand your clients, then design and build something they get right away.

Do you work with clients outside Mexico?

Yes. Most of my clients are in the US, Canada and Europe. Everything is remote, in English or Spanish, across time zones.
