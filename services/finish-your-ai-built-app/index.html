Questions about finishing your app.

I started my app in Lovable or Bolt, can you take it over?

Yes. Those tools are a great way to start. I audit what you built, keep what works and move your product to the technology it needs, so it no longer depends on the tool.

Will you have to rebuild it?

Sometimes, partly or completely. It depends on how it was built and what your product needs to grow. I tell you after the audit, before you commit to anything.

Will I still need my Lovable or Bolt plan?

No. The goal is a product that works on its own, free from those tools’ plans, with code and a domain that are yours.

How much does it cost to finish my app?

It depends on what the audit finds. After a free call and the audit, I send you a fixed-price quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on what’s left to build or rebuild. After the audit you get a clear timeline in the quote.
