Questions about first apps.

What do I need to create my first app?

An idea and the problem it solves. You don’t need a business plan, designs or technical knowledge. On the free call we figure out the rest together.

How much does it cost to build my first app?

It depends on the scope of your MVP, so I quote it at a fixed price after a free call. You’ll know the full investment before we start.

What is an MVP?

The smallest version of your app that solves the main problem for your first users. It lets you launch sooner, spend less and learn from real people.

Mobile app or web app?

It depends on your users. Many first apps start as a web app that works great on phones, and become native apps later. We decide together on the call.

How long does it take?

The UX/UI design of an app usually takes 2–6 weeks. Development time depends on the scope, and you’ll get a clear timeline in the quote.
