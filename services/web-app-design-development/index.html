Questions about web apps.

How much does a web app cost?

It depends on what the app needs to do, so I quote each one at a fixed price after a free call. You’ll know the scope, the timeline and the full investment before we start.

How long does a web app take?

We define the timeline together on the free call, because it depends on the features. The UX/UI design for an app usually takes 2–6 weeks.

Can it take payments?

Yes. I integrate payments with Stripe when your app needs them.

Do you also build the back end?

I design the whole product and build the front-end. If your app needs a more complex back end, I’ll tell you on the first call and plan the best way to handle it with you.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Your code and domain are yours. If you ever want to move, I hand everything over.
